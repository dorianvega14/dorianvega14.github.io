This is my second family recipe

The name of my second family recipe is relleno it is mashed potatoes with ground beef filling with green peas, eggs, olives and carrots formed into a ball with the mashed potatoes as a shell and then fried making it crispy and golden brown.

Ingredients Mesurments
Green Peas 1 tbsp
Mashed Potatoes 3/2 a potatoe
Eggs 1/3 of an egg
Olives 1 olive
Carrots Thin slices
Ground Beef 1 handful

I chose Relleno as my second family recipe because it is really good and it is one of the first ever foods I had here that made me feel like I was home. It reminded me of the time I had it in my Aunts hause for christmas where everyone was happy celebrating and the kids where playing with some of their new toys and everyone looked like they didnt have a worry in the world.
